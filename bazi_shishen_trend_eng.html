Bazi Ten Gods: Favorability in Luck/Annual Pillars & Gender Perspectives

Exploring the profound impact of the Ten Gods on male and female destinies as time flows.

⚠️ Core Bazi Premise: The auspiciousness of a Ten God in a Luck Pillar (Da Yun) or Annual Pillar (Liu Nian) is never absolute.
• When Favorable (Useful God): It brings out positive traits, smoothness, opportunities, and achievements.
• When Unfavorable (Inauspicious God): It amplifies negative traits, bringing pressure, financial loss, and disasters.
• Gender Differences: Male destiny focuses on Wealth (Wife/Finance) and Officer (Career); Female destiny focuses on Officer (Husband), Killings (Alternative romance/Career), and Eating/Hurting Officer (Children/Talent).

Direct Resource

Zheng Yin
✅ Pros
  • Strong learning ability, profound wisdom.
  • Easily receives help from nobles, elders, and mother.
  • Academic success, good reputation, peaceful mindset.
❌ Cons
  • Rich in thought but lacks action.
  • Complacent with the status quo, highly dependent.
  • Lacks pioneering spirit and courage to take risks.
🌟 When Direct Resource is FAVORABLE
MalePromoted by nobles, academic/career success, strong support from elders, dual harvest of reputation and status.
FemaleHarmonious family, loved by mother/elders, good for pregnancy/fertility, inner sense of security.
⚠️ When Direct Resource is UNFAVORABLE
MaleCareer stagnation, heavy dependence, troubled by documents/real estate/mother issues, lack of fighting spirit.
FemaleDepressed thoughts, mother-in-law conflicts (Resource clashes Eating God), lacks independent thought, easily over-interfered by elders.

Indirect Resource

Pian Yin (Owl)
✅ Pros
  • Sharp intuition, strong insight.
  • Excels in niche and highly difficult fields.
  • Possesses unique problem-solving abilities.
❌ Cons
  • Suspicious and sensitive, overthinks.
  • Eccentric personality, extreme thoughts.
  • Fickle, easily gives up halfway. Clashes with Eating God ("Owl Stealing Food").
🌟 When Indirect Resource is FAVORABLE
MaleFame in niche industries, bursts of inspiration, good for certifications/research/metaphysics, solves tricky problems.
FemaleExtremely accurate intuition, good for niche arts/design, rich spiritual world, unique charm.
⚠️ When Indirect Resource is UNFAVORABLE (Owl Stealing Food)
MaleCareer setbacks, financial loss/unemployment, lonely and depressed, easily ambushed by villains, health impaired.
FemaleEmotional depression, gynecological/endocrine issues, bad for children (Owl steals Food), emotional loneliness.

Direct Wealth

Zheng Cai
✅ Pros
  • Strong financial management skills.
  • Down-to-earth, honest, and principled.
  • Good at saving and steadily accumulating assets.
❌ Cons
  • Wealth heavy but body weak, overworks for money.
  • Overvalues money, appears stingy.
  • Easily ignores life's pleasures.
🌟 When Direct Wealth is FAVORABLE
MaleSmooth wealth, promotion/raise; single men meet true love/marry, wife is virtuous and helps husband.
FemaleProper financial management, wealthy family, practical and stable, receives help from female nobles or father.
⚠️ When Direct Wealth is UNFAVORABLE
MaleTroubles caused by wealth, wife gets sick or relationship discord; greedy for wealth ruins reputation.
FemaleExhausted running for money, stingy; high realistic pressure, easily has mother-in-law conflicts (Wealth clashes Resource).

Indirect Wealth

Pian Cai
✅ Pros
  • Flexible business mind, good at seizing opportunities.
  • Wide network, knows how to integrate resources.
  • Entrepreneurial spirit and wealth-creating ability.
❌ Cons
  • Wealth comes and goes, even into debt.
  • Speculation/gambling or blind expansion leads to bankruptcy.
  • Indulges in socializing, alcohol, and sex, damaging health.
🌟 When Indirect Wealth is FAVORABLE
MaleUnexpected wealth, profitable investments; father's health is good; easily meets female confidantes or gets help from romance.
FemaleWide social circle, commercial profits; generous, possesses "strong woman" traits, receives help from male nobles.
⚠️ When Indirect Wealth is UNFAVORABLE
MaleBankruptcy/gambling losses; father's health declines; easily ruined by romance/alcohol/sex.
FemaleExtravagant, unfaithful in love/bad romance; too much wealth ruins Resource, damaging reputation or elders.

Eating God

Shi Shen
✅ Pros
  • Outstanding talent, strong creativity.
  • Gentle personality, optimistic and calm.
  • Knows how to enjoy life, easily generates wealth.
❌ Cons
  • Day Master's strength is further drained.
  • Lacks action and urgency.
  • Indulges in pleasure, easily lazy.
🌟 When Eating God is FAVORABLE
MaleTalent displayed, good food/enjoyment; happy mood, good for investment/wealth generation, blessings arrive.
FemaleStar of blessings, good mood; good for childbirth (Eating God represents daughter), warm and relaxed family.
⚠️ When Eating God is UNFAVORABLE
MaleLazy and decadent, obesity/health issues; ideals detached from reality, talent buried.
FemaleGluttonous and lazy, highly dependent; Eating God transforms into Hurting Officer, easily dissatisfied with marriage/picky about partner.

Hurting Officer

Shang Guan
✅ Pros
  • Smart and witty, strong innovation.
  • Outstanding eloquence, strong persuasiveness.
  • Unafraid of challenges, good at breaking traditions.
❌ Cons
  • Arrogant and aloof, looks down on others.
  • Sharp tongue, easily offends people.
  • Clashes with authority/rules. May trigger "Hurting Officer meets Direct Officer".
🌟 When Hurting Officer is FAVORABLE
MaleInnovative breakthroughs, brilliant talent; good for entrepreneurship/tech/fame, solves industry problems.
FemaleRadiant charm; good for acting/design/freelance; independent thought, breaks conventional bonds.
⚠️ When Hurting Officer is UNFAVORABLE (Meets Direct Officer)
MaleGossip and disputes, arrogance causes trouble; lawsuits, clashes with bosses, career setbacks.
FemaleMajor taboo for marriage: Picky about husband, emotional ups and downs, easily leads to divorce, arguments, or scandalous affairs.

Direct Officer

Zheng Guan
✅ Pros
  • Strong sense of responsibility, self-disciplined.
  • Upright, principled.
  • Easily appreciated by bosses/institutions, builds social status.
❌ Cons
  • Easily feels pressured and constrained.
  • Stereotyped, conservative, timid.
  • Mentally anxious, lacks adaptability.
🌟 When Direct Officer is FAVORABLE
MalePromotion/raise, good for civil service/fame; promoted by nobles, acts openly, status elevates.
FemaleTrue love appears, good for marriage/stable relationship; capable husband, family has status, respected by others.
⚠️ When Direct Officer is UNFAVORABLE
MaleImmense pressure, villains make things difficult; lawsuits, career setbacks, actions restricted.
FemaleEmotionally oppressed, husband is incompetent or brings pressure; strong sense of traditional bondage, easily troubled by romance.

Seven Killings

Qi Sha (Indirect Officer)
✅ Pros
  • Full of boldness, decisive and brave.
  • Strong crisis management and execution.
  • Rises in adversity, excellent leader.
❌ Cons
  • Easily faces villains and external pressure.
  • Mentally tense, lacks security.
  • Easily encounters accidents, injuries, and challenges.
🌟 When Seven Killings is FAVORABLE (Controlled/Generated)
MaleGains power/promotion, full of boldness; good for military/police/pioneering, turns danger into safety, achieves great deeds.
FemaleAttracts high-quality/dominant "CEO" type men; career woman, decisive and capable, receives help from male nobles.
⚠️ When Seven Killings is UNFAVORABLE
MaleAmbushed by villains, accidental disasters; extreme pressure, bad temper causes trouble, easily faces lawsuits/injuries.
FemaleMeets toxic men/bad romance, emotionally hurt; high physical/mental pressure, easily faces gynecological/accidental issues.

Friend Star

Bi Jian
✅ Pros
  • Independent, firm will.
  • Easily receives help from friends, siblings, colleagues.
  • Suitable for teamwork to overcome difficulties.
❌ Cons
  • Obstinate, hard to accept others' opinions.
  • Easily competes with peers.
  • Cooperation may lead to wealth division and resource competition.
🌟 When Friend Star is FAVORABLE
MaleFriends' help, profitable partnerships; firm will, good for teamwork, gets through hardships together.
FemaleHelp from female friends/sisters; independent, confident, does not rely on men.
⚠️ When Friend Star is UNFAVORABLE
MaleFinancial loss/competition, partnership disputes; Clashes Wealth (bad for wife), relationship discord.
FemaleMeets competitors in love (Bi-Jie competing for husband); financial loss, obstinate personality causes interpersonal/marital tension.

Rob Wealth

Jie Cai
✅ Pros
  • Strong will to survive, high competitiveness.
  • Strong social skills, easily gathers network.
  • Good at rallying others for help in difficulties.
❌ Cons
  • Impulsive consumption and investment losses.
  • High risk of bankruptcy, losses due to partnerships.
  • Easily dragged down by relatives/partners.
🌟 When Rob Wealth is FAVORABLE
MaleStrong competitiveness, turnaround in adversity; high social skills, good for market expansion, gets deadly loyalty from peers.
FemaleSoft on outside, tough on inside, strong social skills; can help husband or independently carry burdens, possesses heroic spirit.
⚠️ When Rob Wealth is UNFAVORABLE
MaleSevere financial loss, clashes wife/relationship breakdown; impulsive trouble, betrayed by others, blind investment.
FemaleLove triangle/love stolen; impulsive spending, extreme personality, easily has fierce arguments with partner.

Note: This material is for Bazi learning and reference only. The Ten Gods have no absolute auspiciousness or inauspiciousness. The favorability in Luck and Annual Pillars must be comprehensively analyzed based on the "Strong/Weak Day Master" and the "Useful God" of the original chart.
