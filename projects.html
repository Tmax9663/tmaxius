兩個把硬體帶回人群的專案 Two projects that put hardware back in front of people

做設備工程十年、再自學寫程式之後，我最想做的是把「量測、校正、控制」這套思維，用在人身上 —— 不是工廠裡的機台，而是每個人都會遇到的健康與行動問題。 After ten years in equipment engineering and a second life in software, I want to apply the same discipline — measure, calibrate, control — to people rather than machines: the health and mobility problems everyone eventually meets.

以下兩個專案目前都在概念與研究階段，尚未成為產品，也不是醫療器材。 Both projects below are at concept and research stage: not products, not medical devices.

01 AI 面診 AppAI Facial Diagnosis App

概念與研究Concept & Research

願景　Vision　 用手機前鏡頭掃描臉部，依臉型、氣色（膚色）與五官特徵等視覺線索，結合 AI 與大型醫學／生理資料，提供健康風險的早期提示 —— 讓「看一眼就知道要不要去看醫生」變得更可行。 Use the phone's front camera to scan a face and read visual cues — facial shape, complexion, colour tone and feature patterns — combining AI with large-scale medical and physiological data to surface early-warning signals: making “should I see a doctor?” easier to answer.

做法Approach

  • 取像Capture — 手機鏡頭拍攝，含光線、白平衡與角度校正。 phone capture with lighting, white-balance and angle normalisation.
  • 電腦視覺Computer vision — 臉部地標、臉型與氣色分析，區域層級的變化比對。 facial landmarks, shape and complexion analysis, region-level comparison.
  • AI 模型AI models — 以「臉部特徵 ↔ 健康狀況」的大規模資料訓練。 trained on large datasets correlating facial symptoms with conditions.
  • 輸出Output — 易懂的提示與就醫建議，並附明確醫療免責。 plain-language insights and guidance, with explicit medical disclaimers.

階段目標Next milestones

  • 資料來源與倫理審查（影像、同意、去識別化） data sourcing and ethics review (images, consent, de-identification)
  • 建立可重複的取像標準（光線／白平衡／距離） a repeatable capture standard (light, white balance, distance)
  • 先做「氣色／膚色」單一目的的可行性驗證 feasibility test on one narrow task first (complexion / tone)
  • 法規定位（健康建議 ≠ 診斷） regulatory positioning (guidance ≠ diagnosis)

為什麼是我　Why me　 已自學完成命理類 App（數字命理、八字），從資料到介面的流程熟悉；設備工程累積的圖樣辨識、感測器校正與量測偏差控制，正是「把一張手機照片變成可重複量測」的核心難題。 I have already shipped self-taught apps in the metaphysical domain (Numerology, BaZi), so the data-to-interface pipeline is familiar; equipment engineering contributes pattern recognition, sensor calibration discipline and measurement-variance control — exactly the hard part of turning a phone photo into a repeatable measurement.

02 助行機械腿Mechanical Leg for Mobility

概念Concept

願景　Vision　 為截肢者、長者與腿部功能受損的人設計一只機械腿，幫助他們重新走路、重新自己出門 —— 把獨立還給人，而不是把人留在家裡。 Design and build a mechanical leg that helps amputees, the elderly and people with leg impairments walk again and go out on their own — giving independence back instead of keeping people at home.

做法Approach

  • 系統System — 機械／機器腿，支援行走與日常活動。 a mechanical / robotic leg supporting walking and daily movement.
  • 使用者Users — 截肢者、長者、腿部功能受損者。 amputees, the elderly, people with leg impairments.
  • 整合Integration — 機電整合＋輔助科技設計：致動器、控制、步態感測、安全與舒適。 mechanical-electronic integration plus assistive design: actuators, control, gait sensing, safety, comfort.
  • 目標Goal — 改善生活品質、恢復獨立。 improve quality of life and restore independence.

階段目標Next milestones

  • 使用者訪談：真正卡住的是哪一段路 user interviews: which part of walking actually fails
  • 法規路徑（醫療器材分級與認證） regulatory path (device classification and certification)
  • 機構原型：對位、負重、散熱、電力 mechanical prototype: alignment, load, heat, power
  • 控制：步態感測與安全停止 control: gait sensing and safe-stop behaviour

為什麼是我　Why me　 十年機電設備工程（維護、校正、良率、安全）＋自動化與控制系統經驗可以直接轉用；這是從工業設備走向「以人為本」的工程，最自然的一步。 Ten years of mechanical-electronic equipment engineering — maintenance, calibration, yield, safety — plus automation and control experience transfers directly; this is the natural step from industrial equipment to human-centred engineering.
