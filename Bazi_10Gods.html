八字十神解说Bazi Ten Gods Explanation

从「我」与世界的关系，看懂人际、性格与人生 Understanding interpersonal relationships, personality, and life from the relationship between "Me" and the world

一、什么是十神1. What are the Ten Gods?

这里所说的「十神」，与神鬼没有任何关系。简单来说，十神就是以我们的日元——也就是「我」——为中心，观察「我」与八字中其他五行之间所形成的生克关系。因此，看一个人的性格、思维方式以及人生际遇，不能只看他的日元是什么，也需要观察八字中出现了哪些十神、它们处在什么位置，以及彼此之间的力量和关系。 The "Ten Gods" here have nothing to do with deities or ghosts. Simply put, the Ten Gods use our Day Master (the "Me") as the center to observe the generating and overcoming relationships between "Me" and the other Five Elements in the Bazi chart. Therefore, to understand a person's personality, mindset, and life encounters, we cannot just look at the Day Master; we must also observe which Ten Gods appear, their positions, and their relative strengths and relationships.

十神从哪里来Where do the Ten Gods come from?

十神最有意思的地方，就是它把人与人之间的关系，用五行的生克制化表现出来。简单来说： The most interesting part of the Ten Gods is that it expresses human relationships through the generating and overcoming of the Five Elements. Simply put:

  • 生我者为印——滋养、保护、教育、知识Generates Me is Resource — Nurturing, protection, education, knowledge
  • 我生者为食伤——表达、创造、输出I Generate is Output — Expression, creation, output
  • 我克者为财——现实、资源、责任I Overcome is Wealth — Reality, resources, responsibility
  • 克我者为官杀——规则、责任、压力与约束Overcomes Me is Officer/Killings — Rules, responsibility, pressure, constraints
  • 同我者为比劫——同辈、朋友、竞争Same as Me is Friend/Rob — Peers, friends, competition

没有一个十神是绝对的好，也没有一个十神是绝对的坏。关键不是你有什么十神，而是这个十神在你的八字里面，是不是用得好。 No Ten God is absolutely good or bad. The key is not what Ten Gods you have, but whether they are used well in your specific chart.

三、比肩——我自己来3. Friend (Bi Jian) — I'll do it myself

五行定义：与日元同一种五行，阴阳同性。Definition: Same element as Day Master, same polarity.

比肩顾名思义，就是跟我同肩、同辈的人。代表兄弟姐妹、朋友、同辈、同事，以及与自己能力条件接近的人。「比」也有比较的意思，所以比肩带有比较与竞争的性质。 As the name suggests, Friend represents people on the same level and generation. It symbolizes siblings, friends, peers, colleagues, and those with similar abilities. The character "Bi" also implies comparison, so Friend carries a nature of comparison and competition.

独立、自主、有主见Independent, Autonomous, Opinionated

比肩格的人通常比较有自己的想法，独立、自主、有原则。他们不喜欢受到太多规则和限制。适合个人品牌、自由职业、创业。如果你给他一个明确的目标，放手让他自己去做，他可能做得更加好。 People with strong Friend energy usually have their own ideas, are independent, autonomous, and principled. They dislike excessive rules and restrictions. They are suited for personal branding, freelancing, and entrepreneurship. If given a clear goal and the freedom to execute, they often perform exceptionally well.

自尊心与原则 / 合伙要小心Self-esteem & Principles / Caution in Partnerships

他们非常有自尊心和原则，不会因为别人不认同就轻易改变想法。缺点是容易固执、主观、我行我素。合伙做生意时要特别注意，如果大家都是非常有主见的人，容易把权责和决定权讲清楚，否则朋友容易变成竞争对手。 They possess strong self-esteem and principles, rarely changing their minds just because others disagree. The downside is that they can be stubborn, subjective, and do things their own way. In business partnerships, it's crucial to clarify responsibilities and decision-making power; otherwise, friends can easily become competitors.

四、劫财——大家一起来4. Rob Wealth (Jie Cai) — Let's do it together

五行定义：与日元同一种五行，阴阳异性。Definition: Same element as Day Master, opposite polarity.

如果说比肩比较像「我自己来」，那么劫财比较像「我们一起来」。劫财的人通常比较有行动力，喜欢与人互动、合作、竞争。反应快、胆量大、敢冒险，看到机会就会「先做了再说！」，具有很强的开创精神。 If Friend is like "I'll do it myself," Rob Wealth is like "Let's do it together." People with Rob Wealth are action-oriented, enjoy interacting, cooperating, and competing. They react quickly, are bold, dare to take risks, and will often say "Let's just do it first!" when they see an opportunity, showing strong pioneering spirit.

朋友、人脉与竞争 / 财来财去Friends, Network & Competition / Wealth Comes and Goes

劫财强的人通常不会喜欢一个人孤孤单单地做事情，比较容易认识很多人，懂得利用人脉。但需要注意的是「财来财去」。他们赚钱的能力不一定差，甚至很敢赚钱，但赚得到未必守得住。容易为了朋友、合作、机会而花钱。因此，劫财格的人最好找一个正财格的人来帮他守着财。 Those with strong Rob Wealth dislike working alone; they easily meet many people and know how to leverage networks. However, a key trait is "wealth comes and goes." Their ability to make money is not bad, and they dare to invest, but they may struggle to keep it. They tend to spend on friends, partnerships, or opportunities. Thus, it's best for them to find someone with Direct Wealth to manage their finances.

六、食神——把兴趣变成才华6. Eating God — Turning Interest into Talent

五行定义：我生的五行，阴阳同性。代表才华、兴趣、优雅、享受生活；女命亦代表女儿。Definition: Element I generate, same polarity. Represents talent, interests, elegance, enjoying life; for females, it also represents daughters.

食神格的人能够很自然地把自己的才华发挥出来。在传统命理中，食神格也有「富神格」之称。这类人通常比较懂得享受生活，喜欢美好的事物、美食、音乐、艺术，性格温和、乐观。他们适合从事艺术家、作家、创作者或自由业者等工作，能够把兴趣发挥成才华。 People with Eating God can naturally express their talents. In traditional命理, it is also called the "Rich God" structure. Such people know how to enjoy life, appreciate beauty, food, music, and art, and have a gentle, optimistic character. They are suited for artists, writers, creators, or freelancers, turning their interests into talents.

食神生财：做自己喜欢的事情，把兴趣变成才华，再把才华发挥出来，最后创造财富。以嗜好赚钱，以才华得财。 Eating God generates Wealth: Do what you love, turn interest into talent, express that talent, and ultimately create wealth — "make money from your hobby, gain wealth through your talent."

七、伤官——敢于挑战规则的力量7. Hurting Officer — The Power to Challenge Rules

五行定义：我生的五行，阴阳异性。代表创意、才华、聪明、独特思想、不墨守成规。Definition: Element I generate, opposite polarity. Represents creativity, talent, intelligence, unique thinking, and non-conformity.

顾名思义，「伤官」就是「伤害管理者」。伤官不喜欢被管理、约束，不喜欢受到太多规则的限制。如果女命的伤官坐在夫妻宫，婚姻生活中往往比较容易出现矛盾，因为天生不喜欢被另一半管束。但伤官也代表创意、才华、独特的思想，喜欢 think out of the box。就像一匹千里野马，敢于打破旧有的规则，是创造大事业的重要力量。 Literally "the one who hurts the Officer," Hurting Officer dislikes being managed, restricted, or bound by rules. If a female's Hurting Officer sits in the Spouse Palace, marital conflicts may arise as she naturally dislikes being controlled. Yet, it also represents creativity, talent, and unique thinking, liking to think outside the box. Like a wild horse, it dares to break old rules and is a powerful force for creating great endeavors.

伤官是一把双刃剑。太旺的人往往不太适合朝九晚五、处处受到约束的工作，更适合创业或需要创意、自由发挥的工作。尤其是伤官加七杀，往往更有创业和开创事业的气势。 Hurting Officer is a double-edged sword. When too strong, they are unsuited for rigid 9-to-5 jobs and better fit for entrepreneurship or creative, free-flowing work. Especially when combined with Seven Killings, they have the momentum to pioneer and start businesses.

专题：伤官配印Topic: Hurting Officer paired with Resource

伤官太旺要怎样化解呢？这就是八字里很重要的一句话：伤官配印。印代表智慧、教育、修养与学习。伤官太旺，就需要通过教育、智慧、修养和定力来引导它。真正的智慧，不是把伤官的个性压下去，而是要学会驾驭它。就像一匹千里野马，如果没有缰绳，它可能会横冲直撞；但如果能够驯服它、引导它，它就能够跑得更远。伤官配印的真正意义，就是让它学会——在对的地方奔跑，在对的地方叛逆。 How to resolve an excessively strong Hurting Officer? This brings us to the principle: "Hurting Officer paired with Resource." Resource represents wisdom, education, cultivation, and learning. An overly strong Hurting Officer needs to be guided by wisdom and self-discipline. True wisdom is not to suppress the Hurting Officer's nature, but to learn to harness it. Like a wild horse without reins, it may charge blindly; but if tamed and guided, it can run much further. The true meaning is to teach it to run and rebel in the right places.

专题：食神生财Topic: Eating God generates Wealth

「食神生财」不只是财的运行路线，更是一种人生的运作方式："Eating God generates Wealth" is not just a wealth flow, but a way of operating in life:

  • 第一步：让兴趣变成才华。Step 1: Turn interest into talent.
  • 第二步：让才华创造价值。Step 2: Let talent create value.
  • 第三步：让价值转化为财富。Step 3: Convert value into wealth.

专题：枭神夺食Topic: Indirect Resource suppresses Eating God

所谓「枭神」，就是偏印。古人把偏印比喻成「枭」，因为枭鸟会夺取幼鸟的食物，用来形容偏印克制食神。当一个人的食神受到压制时，才华和行动力可能无法正常发挥，变得被动、缺乏动力、依赖别人。有些人整天没有事情做，就容易胡思乱想，心情低落。化解之道在于「食神生财」，鼓励去做自己喜欢的事情，让兴趣有机会发挥出来，甚至通过才华创造财富。 The "Owl God" refers to Indirect Resource. Ancients compared it to an owl that steals food from young birds, describing how Indirect Resource suppresses Eating God. When suppressed, one's talent and action may fail to express, leading to passivity, lack of motivation, and dependency. People may overthink and feel depressed when idle. The cure lies in "Eating God generates Wealth" — encouraging them to pursue their interests, letting their talents shine, and ultimately creating wealth through their hobbies.

十一、正财——稳定踏实的财务头脑11. Direct Wealth — The Steady & Disciplined Financial Mind

五行定义：我克的五行，阴阳异性。代表固定收入、俸禄、正妻、储蓄、原则。Definition: Element I overcome, opposite polarity. Represents fixed income, salary, lawful wife, savings, principles.

正财好比一个人的俸禄，那是固定的收入。性格特质：稳定踏实、节俭、重视储蓄、为人正派、做事有原则、不爱冒险。但有时因为太重视金钱，会显得冷淡无情，缺乏人生乐趣，太过保守。正财格的男性比较喜欢年长的女性。大老板通常会请正财格的人来当财务总监。 Direct Wealth is like a person's salary, a fixed income. Personality: Grounded, frugal, values savings, honest, principled, dislikes risks. However, they can sometimes appear emotionally distant due to an over-focus on money, lacking life's joys, and being overly conservative. Men with Direct Wealth often prefer older partners. Bosses usually hire them as CFOs.

十二、偏财——豪爽大方的生意奇才12. Indirect Wealth — The Generous Business Genius

五行定义：我克的五行，阴阳同性。代表不固定收入、生意、大财、情人、交际。Definition: Element I overcome, same polarity. Represents variable income, business, large wealth, lovers, socializing.

偏财是不固定的收入，遇到好时机就可能赚大钱。对赚钱嗅觉灵敏，看重的是大财。性格豪爽大方，江湖气十足，交际手腕好。财来得快去得快，善用机会赚钱，喜欢独创事业。处理事情圆滑机智，喜欢与人谈笑风生。偏财多的男人多数有情人，因为他们幽默、容易吸引异性。他们不吝啬，日子过得很快活——work smart, play hard。 Indirect Wealth is variable income; they can make big money when the timing is right. Sharp business instinct, playing for high stakes. Generous, charismatic, excellent networkers. Easy come, easy go. They seize opportunities, love entrepreneurship, and handle things tactfully. Men with much Indirect Wealth often have mistresses, as their humor attracts romance. They are not stingy and live by "work smart, play hard."

专题：为什么说「财多身子弱」？Topic: Why "Too Much Wealth Makes a Weak Body"?

如果八字里财多，这个八字一般上是比较弱的（身弱）。因为如果日元力量都去克其他元素了，力量就已经消耗了。常见呈现方式：1. 健康透支（努力工作耗尽健康）；2. 纵欲伤身（男命财为女人，好色弄垮身体）；3. 资产富有，现金贫穷（看起来有钱，住大房子，但都是不动产，不是现金）。 When there is too much Wealth in a chart, the Day Master's energy becomes heavily depleted (Weak Body). This shows up in three main ways: 1. Health Burnout (working so hard you ruin your health); 2. Indulgence (since Wealth represents women for men, overindulgence harms physical well-being); 3. Asset Rich, Cash Poor (looking wealthy with big assets, but lacking liquid cash).

十四、正官——规矩与秩序14. Direct Officer — Rules and Order

五行定义：克我的五行，阴阳异性。代表规则、秩序、管理、责任、名分；女命亦代表丈夫。Definition: Element that overcomes Me, opposite polarity. Represents rules, order, management, responsibility, status; for females, it represents the husband.

正官像一位管理者或文官，最重要的四个字：规矩、秩序。正官格的人通常服从规矩，做事稳重、可靠，不喜欢做出格的事情。有责任感，分辨是非，适合做主管、管理层、公务员。但正官太旺也有问题：太重视规矩，太在意别人眼光，活得很累。最大的优点是「稳」，最大的缺点也是「太稳」，容易优柔寡断、错过时机。 Direct Officer is like an administrator or civil official; the core is rules and order. They are stable, reliable, law-abiding, and suited for management or public service. However, too much Direct Officer makes them overly concerned with appearances and others' opinions, living a respectable but tiring life. Their greatest strength is stability; their greatest weakness is being too stable, leading to indecisiveness and missed opportunities.

十五、七杀——魄力与突破15. Seven Killings — Courage and Breakthrough

五行定义：克我的五行，阴阳同性。代表魄力、积极、竞争、突破、开创、压力。Definition: Element that overcomes Me, same polarity. Represents boldness, initiative, competition, breakthrough, pioneering, pressure.

如果正官是文官，七杀就是一位将军。面对战场，必须马上决定、马上行动。七杀的人行动力强，性格直接，雷厉风行，有竞争心，不怕困难。七杀也代表压力，但压力迫使一个人成长。在竞争激烈时更能发挥实力。但七杀也是一把双刃剑，容易太强势、冲动、爱面子。适合军警、管理、创业等需要勇气和决断的工作。 If Direct Officer is a civil official, Seven Killings is a general. Facing the battlefield, decisions must be immediate. They are action-oriented, direct, competitive, and unafraid of difficulties. Seven Killings also represents pressure, which often forces growth. They perform best under intense competition. But it's a double-edged sword: can become too aggressive, impulsive, or face-saving. Suited for military, police, management, and entrepreneurship requiring courage and decisiveness.

十七、正印——母亲、滋养、保护与无条件的支持17. Direct Resource — The Mother, Nurturing, Protection and Unconditional Support

五行定义：生我的五行，阴阳异性。代表母亲、师长、贵人、知识、文化、修养、精神世界。Definition: Element that generates Me, opposite polarity. Represents mother, teachers, benefactors, knowledge, culture, cultivation, spiritual world.

正印可以说是最好的十神，也是智慧的代表。母亲就像太阳与大地，给予生命、养育、保护、教育。正印明显的人容易得到长辈、贵人的帮助，重视精神层面的满足，善良、温和、有涵养。但正印太强，如果保护过度，可能表现为依赖、顾虑较多、行动前思考太久。真正好的正印，是先给予养分，再让他走自己的路。 Direct Resource is often considered the best Ten God, representing wisdom. A mother is like the sun and earth, giving life, nurturing, and protecting. Those with strong Direct Resource easily receive help from elders and benefactors, value spiritual fulfillment, and are kind, gentle, and cultured. However, if overly protected, it can lead to dependency, overthinking, and hesitation. True Direct Resource gives nourishment first, then lets them walk their own path.

十八、偏印——不按常理出牌的技能大师18. Indirect Resource — The Unconventional Skill Master

五行定义：生我的五行，阴阳同性。代表养母、继母、偏门知识、玄学、宗教、艺术、技能。Definition: Element that generates Me, same polarity. Represents adoptive/step mother, unconventional knowledge, metaphysics, religion, arts, skills.

如果正印是母亲，偏印就是养母、继母。正印给温暖，偏印给技能。偏印的人脑子转得特别快，对偏门知识、玄学、宗教、艺术特别有缘。他们善变，喜欢弃旧立新，不按部就班，具有个人主义色彩。警戒心强，观察力敏锐，学习玄学是一把手。但容易钻牛角尖、思考过度、没有耐心。 If Direct Resource is the birth mother, Indirect Resource is the adoptive/step mother. Direct Resource gives warmth; Indirect Resource gives skills. They have razor-sharp minds, naturally excelling in metaphysics, religion, psychology, and arts. They dislike following rules, are often seen as eccentric visionaries, and have keen observation. However, they tend to overthink, hesitate, and lack patience.

十九、从日柱看「我」和我的另一半19. Day Pillar: "Me" and My Spouse

八字里面，日柱非常重要。日元代表「我」，而日支则是距离日元最近的位置，传统命理中常用来观察夫妻宫，也就是我们与另一半之间的关系。男命通常以财星观察妻星，女命则通常以官杀观察夫星。例如男命日支坐正财，可以从这个组合去观察他与妻子的缘分。提醒：这些都不能单独看一个位置就下定论，必须结合整个八字。 In Bazi, the Day Pillar is extremely important. The Day Master represents "Me", and the Day Branch is the closest position, traditionally used to observe the Spouse Palace (the relationship with the partner). For males, Wealth represents the wife; for females, Officer/Killings represents the husband. For example, if a male's Day Branch sits on Direct Wealth, we can observe his affinity with his wife. Reminder: These cannot be judged by a single position alone; the entire chart must be considered.

二十一、十神：一张人生关系图21. Ten Gods: A Map of Life Relationships

十神把人与人之间的关系，用五行的生克制化表现出来。例如「财克印」：男命的财星代表妻子，印星代表母亲。财与印之间的「克」，经常用来解释家庭关系中妻子与母亲之间可能出现的不同立场（婆媳问题）。另外，正印也常被视为一个人的良知、原则。当财星过旺而强力克印时，也可以引申为一个人过度追求现实利益，而忽略了内在原则与良知。 The Ten Gods express human relationships through the Five Elements. For example, "Wealth overcomes Resource": For a male, Wealth represents the wife, and Resource represents the mother. The "overcoming" between them often explains the potential conflict between wife and mother (mother-in-law issues). Also, Direct Resource represents one's conscience and principles. When Wealth is too strong and overcomes Resource, it can imply that a person pursues material benefits excessively, ignoring their inner conscience and principles.

二十三、结语：知己知彼23. Conclusion: Know Yourself, Know Others

八字不是用来给自己贴标签，而是让我们更了解自己。知道自己的性格以后，懂得发挥自己的长处，也懂得修正自己的弱点。能够知己，也能够知人。这就是孙子兵法里面的「知己知彼，百战不殆」。学八字，其实就是学会从「我」出发，看懂我与父母、伴侣、子女、朋友、社会，以及这个世界之间的关系。当你真正看懂十神，你看的就不再只是一个人的命盘，你看到的，其实是一幅人生关系图。 Bazi is not meant to label ourselves, but to help us understand ourselves better. Knowing our personality, we can leverage our strengths and correct our weaknesses. To know yourself and to know others — this is the essence of "Know yourself, know your enemy, and you will never be defeated" from The Art of War. Learning Bazi is essentially learning to start from "Me" and understand the relationships with parents, partners, children, friends, society, and the world. When you truly understand the Ten Gods, you are no longer just looking at a chart; you are looking at a map of life relationships.
