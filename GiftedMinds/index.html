FAQ

Questions parents often ask

What does an intervention centre actually do?
We teach children the skills that don't come easily to them — reading, numbers, attention, communication, self-regulation — using structured, multisensory methods, in 1-to-1 or small-group sessions. Think of it as specialist coaching for learning, not regular tuition.
Does my child need a formal diagnosis first?
No. If your child is struggling, support can begin now — you don't need to wait for a label. During our consultation we'll help you understand your child's profile, and if a formal assessment is useful, we can point you to qualified professionals in Penang.
Do you treat or cure ADHD / autism?
We are an educational intervention centre, not a medical provider. We don't "cure" — and we don't believe children need fixing. We build practical skills, strategies and confidence, and work alongside parents and any therapists already supporting your child.
What age can my child start?
Early support makes a big difference — readiness skills can begin in the preschool years, and it's never too late for older students. WhatsApp us your child's age and situation and we'll advise the best starting point.
My child has more than one learning difference. Can you help?
Yes — overlapping profiles (for example dyslexia + ADHD) are very common. That's exactly why every plan at Gifted Minds is personalised rather than one-size-fits-all.
How do I enrol or find out about fees?
The fastest way is WhatsApp: 016-437 6808. Tell us a little about your child and we'll arrange a consultation and explain programme options and fees.
